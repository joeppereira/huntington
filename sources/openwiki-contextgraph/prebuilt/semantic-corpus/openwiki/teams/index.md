# Files

- [Card Technology](card-technology.md) - Card Technology is the engineering team that owns the Card Management API (API-06, v2.6, /cards/v2) in the Card Services domain of the Developer Platform. This page covers what the team is accountable for, who depends on its API, and which operating commitments it carries.
- [Consumer & Wholesale Credit Risk - Allowance Methodology](consumer-wholesale-credit-risk-allowance-methodology.md) - The team that owns the Tier 1 CECL Lifetime Expected Credit Loss Model (MDL-CR-007) at Meridian Harbor Financial Corp. It is accountable for the model's inputs, scenario weighting, qualitative overlay and the reconciliation of the allowance to the reported $15,920 million.
- [Corporate Treasury - Asset & Liability Management](corporate-treasury-alm.md) - The Corporate Treasury sub-team at Meridian Harbor Financial Corp. (a fictional bank) that owns the Tier 1 NII Sensitivity Model MDL-ALM-014. Covers what the team is accountable for, its data dependencies, governance counterparts, limits and the operating duties that follow from model ownership.
- [Corporate Treasury - Capital Management](corporate-treasury-capital-management.md) - Treasury/CIO sub-team of Meridian Harbor Financial Corp. that owns MDL-CAP-003, the Tier 1 Capital Planning & Stress Projection Model, which produces the nine-quarter CET1, RWA and capital-ratio plan used to size dividends and buybacks.
- [Credit Platforms Engineering](credit-platforms-engineering.md) - Credit Platforms Engineering is the team that owns the Credit Decisioning API (API-09, v2.2, /credit-decisions/v2) in the Credit Origination domain. This page covers what the team is accountable for, who depends on its API, the upstream services it relies on, and the operating commitments it carries.
- [Developer Platform Engineering](developer-platform-engineering.md) - Developer Platform Engineering owns the Webhooks & Event Notifications API (API-18, v1.3, /events/v1) and the Developer Platform API Reference document (v3.6) that sets conventions for all 18 Meridian Harbor APIs. This page covers what the team is accountable for, what it does not own, and the commitments it carries.
- [Digital Platforms Engineering](digital-platforms-engineering.md) - Digital Platforms Engineering owns three Developer Platform APIs - Accounts (API-01), Transactions (API-02) and Statements & Documents (API-17) - which supply balances, transactions and documents to the mobile app, online banking, corporate clients and aggregators. This page covers what the team is accountable for, the commitments it carries, how its APIs depend on each other, and what the sources leave unsaid.
- [Finance Technology](finance-technology.md) - Finance Technology is the engineering team that owns the Regulatory Reporting API (API-16, v1.8, /regulatory/v1), the governed source of regulatory capital, RWA, leverage exposure and report line items. This page covers its accountabilities, commitments, consumers and operating constraints.
- [Fraud Strategy Engineering](fraud-strategy-engineering.md) - Fraud Strategy Engineering is the team that owns the Fraud Risk Signals API (API-08, v4.0, /fraud/v4) in the Fraud & Financial Crimes domain. This page covers what the team is accountable for, who depends on its API, its upstream data, and the operating commitments it carries.
- [Global Payments Technology](global-payments-technology.md) - Global Payments Technology owns the three money-movement APIs of the Meridian Harbor Developer Platform, API-03 Payments Initiation, API-04 Real-Time Payments and API-05 Wire Transfer. This page covers what the team is accountable for, how its APIs differ, their shared dependencies and the commitments they carry.
- [Know-Your-Customer Platform](kyc-platform.md) - The Know-Your-Customer Platform team owns the Customer Identity & KYC API (API-07, v1.9, /kyc/v1) in the Client Onboarding domain. This page covers what the team is accountable for, who depends on its API, and the commitments it carries.
- [Lending Platforms Engineering](lending-platforms-engineering.md) - Lending Platforms Engineering owns the Loan Servicing API (API-11, v2.8, /loans/v2) in the Lending Operations domain. This page covers what the team is accountable for, who depends on its API (including CECL and ALM models), its upstream inputs, and the operating and change-control obligations it carries.
- [Market Data Services](market-data-services.md) - The team that owns the Market Data API (API-12) and the FX Rates API (API-13) in the Markets & Treasury domain; covers its two services, downstream dependents, governance obligations and operating expectations.
- [Model Risk Governance & Review (MRGR)](model-risk-governance-review.md) - Independent model validation function of Meridian Harbor Financial Corp. that reports to the Chief Risk Officer, validates the three Tier 1 models (MDL-ALM-014, MDL-CR-007, MDL-CAP-003), approves material model changes and reviews breaking API changes that affect them.
- [Risk Analytics Engineering](risk-analytics-engineering.md) - Risk Analytics Engineering owns two critical data services on the Developer Platform, the Credit Risk Scoring API (API-10) and the Macroeconomic Scenario API (API-14). Together they supply the PD/LGD parameters and the approved scenario sets that feed the CECL model and capital planning.
- [Treasury Technology](treasury-technology.md) - The team that owns the Treasury Liquidity Positions API (API-15) in the Treasury domain; covers what the service publishes, who consumes it, its service levels, access restrictions and the governance that follows from its critical-data-service status.
