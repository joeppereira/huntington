# Files

- [Capital Baseline Projection](capital-baseline-projection.md) - The Baseline_Projection sheet of the Capital Planning & Stress Projection Model (MDL-CAP-003). It rolls CET1 capital, RWA, share count and capital headroom forward nine quarters (Q3 2026 to Q3 2028) under the firm's baseline plan.
- [Capital Stress Projection](capital-stress-projection.md) - The Stress_Projection sheet of MDL-CAP-003, which rolls CET1 capital and RWA forward nine quarters under the severely adverse scenario and yields the minimum stressed CET1 ratio (12.90% in Q3 2027) that drives the indicative stress capital buffer and the pass/fail check against the regulatory requirement.
- [CECL LGD and EAD](cecl-lgd-and-ead.md) - How the MDL-CR-007 CECL workbook derives scenario-conditional loss given default (LGD) from base LGD and collateral price paths, takes exposure at default (EAD) from loan-servicing balances, and multiplies them with lifetime PD in ECL_Calc.
- [CECL Lifetime PD](cecl-lifetime-pd.md) - How the CECL allowance model (MDL-CR-007) turns a pool-level annual PD into a scenario-conditional lifetime probability of default, using an unemployment-elasticity shock anchored on the baseline scenario and compounding over remaining life. Covers formulas, inputs, resulting values, invariants and limits.
- [CECL Qualitative Overlay](cecl-qualitative-overlay.md) - The per-segment management adjustment (Allowance_Summary!F6:F11) added to the probability-weighted modeled CECL allowance in MDL-CR-007. It totals $681.09 million, is a hard-coded input approved by the Allowance Committee, and is checked against a ±15%-of-modeled limit.
- [CECL Scenario Weighting](cecl-scenario-weighting.md) - How the CECL allowance model (MDL-CR-007) combines the Upside, Baseline and Downside lifetime ECL of each segment into one probability-weighted modeled allowance, using the 20/50/30 weights in Scenarios!B6:B8. Covers cells, formulas, weight-sum check, resulting values, sensitivity to 100% single-scenario weighting, and change risks.
- [EVE Modified Duration](eve-modified-duration.md) - How the NII Sensitivity Model (MDL-ALM-014) approximates the change in economic value of equity (EVE) for parallel rate shocks using a per-line modified-duration (dollar duration) calculation. Covers formulas, inputs, FY2025 results, limit test, and the approximation's limits.
- [Indicative Stress Capital Buffer](indicative-stress-capital-buffer.md) - The Summary-sheet calculation in MDL-CAP-003 that turns the stressed CET1 decline plus four quarters of planned dividends into an indicative stress capital buffer (3.18% in the Q2 2026 run), floored at 2.5%, and compares it with the current regulatory requirement.
- [NII Rate Shock Projection (MDL-ALM-014)](nii-rate-shock-projection.md) - The NII_Projection sheet of the NII Sensitivity Model (MDL-ALM-014): how balances, repricing shares and betas turn parallel rate shocks into 12-month change in net interest income, including the -200 bp result of -3,016.89 $mm (-6.01%).
- [NII Repricing and Deposit Betas (MDL-ALM-014)](nii-repricing-and-deposit-betas.md) - How the Net Interest Income Sensitivity Model (MDL-ALM-014) turns repricing-bucket shares, bucket-midpoint timing and rate/deposit betas into per-line repricing factors and per-shock NII effects, with the sheet names, cell addresses, formulas and FY2025 values.
