# Eleanor V. Ashcombe, Chairman and CEO

Eleanor V. Ashcombe is the Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of [Meridian Harbor Financial Corp.](../organizations/meridian-harbor-financial-corp.md) (MHFC). MHFC is a **fictional** financial holding company headquartered in Charlotte Bay, NC. The source documents are labelled synthetic, so everything about Ashcombe and the firm is invented.

The corpus contains no biography for her. It has no tenure, background or compensation data. What it records is her signature and voice in two firm publications:

- **The Letter to Shareholders** in the [2025 Annual Report](../reports/annual-report-2025.md), signed "Chairman and Chief Executive Officer".
- **A quoted statement** in the Q2 2026 earnings supplement, attributed to "Chairman and CEO".

## Role and reporting lines

Ashcombe holds the combined Chairman and CEO roles. Two other executives are named in the source material:

- **Chief Risk Officer.** Dana K. Whitfield reports to the CEO and to the Board Risk Committee. She leads the independent risk function, which is organized by risk stripe (credit, market, liquidity, model, operational, compliance and reputational). This is stated in the Pillar 3 disclosures.
- **Chief Technology Officer.** Marcus O. Lindqvist oversees the Developer Platform and its eighteen production APIs, according to the annual report. The report does not say who he reports to.

The annual report separately says the Board approves the Capital Policy and risk appetite. A Capital Governance Committee chaired by the Chief Financial Officer oversees capital planning. The CFO is not named in the sources.

## Public positions and priorities (2025 shareholder letter)

The letter reflects the leadership themes below. Figures are the firm's reported 2025 results.

| Theme | What the letter says |
|---|---|
| Diversification | The letter describes MHFC as a "balanced, diversified franchise" and says diversification is "the reason we can keep investing through every part of the cycle". |
| Results | Record revenue of $86.1 billion, up 5.2%. Net income was $25.0 billion, or $16.93 per diluted share. ROTCE was 26.4% against a 17% through-the-cycle target. |
| Capital strength | CET1 was 15.1%, against a 10.2% requirement and a 13.0% management target. The letter calls the balance sheet the "first line of defense". It says the firm will hold more capital and liquidity than it strictly needs. $17.1 billion was returned to shareholders. |
| Resilience | "We run the company to be resilient in a severe recession and to keep serving clients when others pull back." |
| Technology and data | $16.8 billion of technology spend. Eighteen production APIs serve clients and also feed risk models for credit-loss allowance, interest rate risk and stress capital projection. Ashcombe describes one set of well-controlled data pipes for clients and regulators as a key 2025 achievement. |
| Risk and controls | She names geopolitical tension, sticky inflation, elevated asset prices and rapid AI adoption as risks. She commits to stronger model risk management, cyber defenses, third-party oversight and payment-system resilience. |

She also highlights business-level developments:

- **Commercial & Investment Bank.** In January 2025 the corporate and investment banking business was combined with commercial banking into a single [Commercial & Investment Bank](../organizations/commercial-investment-bank.md).
- **Consumer & Community Banking.** The segment added 1.9 million net new checking accounts and 61.4 million active digital users. It opened 112 branches and closed 64, a shift toward advice-led branches. See [Consumer & Community Banking](../organizations/consumer-community-banking.md).
- **Asset & Wealth Management.** Client assets reached $3.18 trillion, on record long-term net inflows of $148 billion. See [Asset & Wealth Management](../organizations/asset-wealth-management.md).
- **Workforce.** She thanks the firm's 214,600 employees.

## Q2 2026 statement

In the Q2 2026 earnings supplement Ashcombe is quoted saying the businesses "performed well", clients are active, credit is behaving as expected and the balance sheet "has never been stronger". She adds that the firm remains "alert to a wide range of economic outcomes". That quarter's supplement reports net income of $6.54 billion, EPS of $4.48 and a Standardized CET1 ratio of 15.1%.

Management expectations stated in the 2025 report, such as 2026 net interest income of about $50.2 billion and a Card net charge-off rate of about 3.6%, appear in the MD&A. They are not attributed to Ashcombe personally. The firm presents them as forward-looking management statements.
