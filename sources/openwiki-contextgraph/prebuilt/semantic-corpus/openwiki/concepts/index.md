# Files

- [Basel III Pillar 3](basel-iii-pillar-3.md) - Explains the Basel III Pillar 3 market-disclosure regime and how Meridian Harbor Financial Corp. (a fictional bank) structures its FY2025 Pillar 3 report, including scope, controls, section map, and data lineage.
- [BCBS 239](bcbs-239.md) - How Meridian Harbor Financial Corp. applies the BCBS 239 risk data aggregation and risk reporting principles through "critical data service" APIs on its Developer Platform, including the API-to-model-to-disclosure lineage, governance controls and change management trigger.
- [CECL](cecl.md) - Current Expected Credit Loss (ASC 326), the accounting standard that governs how Meridian Harbor Financial Corp. (a fictional bank) sizes its allowance for credit losses, including the lifetime-loss method, scenario weighting, overlays, governance, data feeds and disclosure.
- [Economic Value of Equity (EVE)](economic-value-of-equity.md) - Defines economic value of equity (EVE) as the value-based IRRBB measure at Meridian Harbor Financial Corp. (a fictional bank), how MDL-ALM-014 approximates it with modified duration, the Board limit, and the FY2025 reported shock sensitivities.
- [G-SIB Surcharge](gsib-surcharge.md) - The G-SIB surcharge is the additional CET1 buffer Meridian Harbor Financial Corp. (MHFC) carries as a global systemically important bank. The reports state it as 2.5%, and it is one component of the 10.2% Standardized CET1 requirement.
- [IRRBB (Interest Rate Risk in the Banking Book)](irrbb.md) - How Meridian Harbor Financial Corp. defines and measures interest rate risk in the banking book through 12-month NII sensitivity and EVE under parallel rate shocks, with the YE2025 results, Board limits, the MDL-ALM-014 model, and the teams and committees that govern it.
- [Model Risk (SR 11-7)](model-risk-sr-11-7.md) - How Meridian Harbor Financial Corp. governs model risk under its SR 11-7-aligned Model Risk Policy, including Tier 1 tiering, MRGR independent validation, monitoring and change-review triggers, applied to the NII sensitivity, CECL and capital planning models.
- [Stress Capital Buffer](stress-capital-buffer.md) - What the stress capital buffer (SCB) is at Meridian Harbor Financial Corp., how it feeds the CET1 requirement stack, and how the Capital Planning & Stress Projection Model (MDL-CAP-003) computes an indicative SCB.
