# Files

- [Fedwire Funds Service](fedwire-funds-service.md) - The Federal Reserve's real-time gross settlement rail for domestic US wires: ISO 20022 messaging since 2025-07-14, operating windows/cutoffs, and the legal meaning of a pacs.002 acknowledgment as final settlement rather than beneficiary credit confirmation.
- [Hold Reason Taxonomy (HRC) and Disclosure Tiers](hold-reason-taxonomy-disclosure-tiers.md) - The 12 HRC hold-reason codes used by the Hold Management Service, their P/C/G/R client-disclosure tiers, and the POL-FCC-014 rules an agent must check any held-wire customer-communication design against before it ships.
- [IMAD and OMAD: Fedwire message identifiers](imad-omad.md) - Explains the Fedwire Input/Output Message Accountability Data identifiers (IMAD, OMAD), which component assigns and carries each one through PRISM Payments Hub (PPH), and why OMAD's absence from the v1 API blocks a true domestic settlement-status feature.
- [ISO 20022 Payment Messages at CNB](iso20022-payment-messages.md) - Reference for the ISO 20022 message types (pacs.008/009/004/002, camt.056/110/111, admi.002, pain.001) used across PRISM Payments Hub and the Payment Network Gateway, and which systems produce and consume each.
- [Settlement, Release, and 'Completed' - Status Semantics](settlement-release-completed-semantics.md) - Explains the unresolved three-way disagreement between PRISM Payments Hub's internal lifecycle model, FCC's client-communication policy, and Crestline Business Online's displayed labels over what "Completed" is allowed to mean for a wire, and how that gap produced CMP-2026-1189.
- [SWIFT gpi & CBPR+](swift-gpi-cbpr.md) - The international wire rail at Crestline National Bank: CBPR+ ISO 20022 pacs.008 mandatory since 2025-11-22, the gpi ACSP/ACCC/RJCT status vocabulary and its tracking-continuation implications, and the observed Q3-2025 outcome distribution (88% ACCC, 8% ACSP/G001, 2.5% pending, 1.5% RJCT, median release-to-ACCC 2h 41min).
- [UETR (Unique End-to-End Transaction Reference)](uetr.md) - Swift gpi's end-to-end tracking identifier, assigned by PRISM Payments Hub (PPH) at release as a UUID v4 (ADR-PAY-023), carried on both Swift CBPR+ and Fedwire pacs.008 messages, and the join key linking PPH v2 identifiers/events, the batch gpi Tracker snapshot, and TDIP - but entirely absent from the legacy v1 API.
- [Wire Lifecycle State Model](wire-lifecycle-state-model.md) - Defines the ten canonical PRISM Payments Hub (PPH) v2 wire states from RECEIVED through RETURNED and shows precisely which of those states the legacy v1 status value PROCESSED conflates, so status-related features and incident analysis can reason about what a status actually confirms.
