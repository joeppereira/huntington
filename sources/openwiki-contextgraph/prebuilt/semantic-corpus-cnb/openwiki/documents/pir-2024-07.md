## Summary

**PIR-2024-07** is the Digital Treasury Channels post-implementation review covering the **Wire
Status Lite** pilot (tracked as **CBO-3120**) and the production incident it caused,
**INC-2024-1182**. The pilot auto-refreshed wire status in Crestline Business Online (CBO) by
polling the Payment Processing Hub (PPH) v1 status API from the browser every 30 seconds for 60
clients between January and May 2024. On 2024-05-31 (month-end), combined pilot and production
refresh traffic drove roughly 85 TPS against the v1 status API, exhausting its thread pool and
delaying wire release for 47 minutes. The pilot was terminated and the epic closed. This page
records the document's identity and governance metadata only; the full incident narrative,
timeline, and remediation detail live on the [Incident page](../incidents/pir-2024-07.md).

## What this document covers

PIR-2024-07 is organized into five numbered sections:

1. **Summary** — the pilot's design (30-second client-side polling of PPH v1 status for 60
   clients), the month-end trigger event, and the outcome (pilot terminated, epic closed).
2. **Impact** — quantified business impact: 1,240 wires delayed, 312 released after the 6:00 p.m.
   ET Fedwire customer cutoff (value next day), 14 clients compensated via interest claims
   totaling $41,800, and a determination that regulatory notification was not required because no
   data exposure occurred.
3. **What we learned about clients** — user-research findings from the pilot, including that 37%
   of surveyed pilot users believed a "Processed" status meant the beneficiary had received the
   funds, which clients valued most (confirmation the wire left CNB, the Fed reference for the
   beneficiary, and international credit timing), and that Treasury Support could not explain wire
   holds to clients without Financial Crimes Compliance (FCC) guidance.
4. **Root causes** — three causes: a polling architecture against a shared synchronous API with no
   client-side backoff; no capacity test at month-end volumes; and status semantics exposed to
   clients without prior validation by Compliance or Legal.
5. **Actions** — the resulting remediation and follow-up work (see below).

## Remediation actions

| Action | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Rate-limit PPH v1 status API to 20 TPS shared | PPH | Done 2024-06 |
| Adopt event-driven status pattern (became ADR-PAY-019) | EA | Done 2024-06 |
| Build a reusable status projection service in CBO | CBO Platform | Delivered for ACH in 2025 (CBO-3815) |
| Validate client-facing status terminology with FCC and Legal before any future tracking feature | CBO Product | Open — carried forward |

One action remains open and carried forward as of the document's last review: validating
client-facing status terminology with FCC and Legal before any future client-facing tracking
feature ships, directly responding to the finding that clients misunderstood the "Processed"
status.

## Why this document exists

PIR-2024-07 exists to formally close out the Wire Status Lite pilot and INC-2024-1182, converting
an incident response into durable organizational learning: it documents quantified client and
business impact, root causes spanning architecture (synchronous polling with no backoff),
operational readiness (no month-end capacity testing), and governance (status semantics not
vetted with Compliance or Legal), and tracks the resulting actions through to either completion
or explicit carry-forward. Its "Related documents" linkage to ADR-PAY-019 makes it the
origin-of-record for the no-polling architectural constraint later enforced across CBO Wire
Center designs.
