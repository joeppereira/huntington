# Files

- [beneficiary_behavior_profile Feature Table](beneficiary-behavior-profile.md) - Restricted - Client Confidential TDIP feature table, keyed on on-us beneficiary account x day, that derives mule-risk behavioral features for the Tier 1 model M-FCT-0034; use is limited to FCT fraud/mule detection and is barred from product or client-facing purposes.
- [Dataset: cbo_wire_events](cbo-wire-events.md) - Internal-classified Treasury Data & Insights Platform (TDIP) dataset that ingests hourly clickstream events from the Crestline Business Online (CBO) Wire Center module, retained for a 13-month rolling history.
- [client_hierarchy Dataset](client-hierarchy.md) - Daily TDIP dataset, sourced from CIF and the Commercial Entitlements Service, that links clients to accounts (current plus two years of history) as the entitlement join key for payments analytics; classified Confidential - Client.
- [client_wire_history_features Feature Table](client-wire-history-features.md) - Prototype, Confidential - Client TDIP feature table at originating-client x beneficiary grain that summarizes a client's own wire completion history (count, median and p90 release-to-ACCC, last_seen); unproductionized, unregistered in MRM, and the closest existing asset to a client-facing "typical completion time" feature.
- [dda_txn_history Dataset](dda-txn-history.md) - Daily TDIP feed of on-us deposit account transaction history from the Core Deposit Platform, retained for 7 years and classified Restricted - Client Confidential, the most sensitive classification tier and sole FCT-only dataset in the Payments domain catalog.
- [fed_ack_events (not ingested)](fed-ack-events.md) - Planned Treasury Data & Insights Platform (TDIP) dataset for Fed acceptance / OMAD acknowledgement events from net.fedwire.ack.v1; currently not ingested and blocked in backlog as TDA-2210.
- [Dataset: gpi_tracker_events](gpi-tracker-events.md) - TDIP payments-domain table that copies Swift gpi tracking data from the GPI_TRACKER_SNAPSHOT Oracle table into Snowflake for analytics; Confidential - Client, history since 2025-03-01, moving from nightly to hourly refresh under TDA-2188.
- [Dataset: GPI_TRACKER_SNAPSHOT (Oracle table)](gpi-tracker-snapshot.md) - Oracle table owned by the Swift Alliance & gpi Connector (GPI-C) that holds the single source of Swift gpi tracking status for outbound wires, refreshed every 4 hours and never exposed directly to client channels.
- [Dataset: pay_wire_txn_hist](pay-wire-txn-hist.md) - Confidential - Client Treasury Data & Insights Platform (TDIP) dataset that captures PRISM Payments Hub (PPH) wire transactions via hourly CDC with 7 years of history; the base wire-transaction source behind all three Payments-domain feature tables, though its uetr column is populated only for Swift wires since 2018 and Fedwire wires since 2025-10, and it carries no authoritative Fed settlement timestamp.
- [wire_corridor_stats_daily Feature Table](wire-corridor-stats-daily.md) - Internal-classified TDIP feature table at currency x destination-country x day grain, built for Payment Ops dashboards, that aggregates wire counts, distinct originating clients, same-day ACCC rate and median hours to ACCC across all originating clients; its sample rows double as a worked example of DUS-07's client-facing cohort-aggregation thresholds, which thin corridors such as NGN/NG fail.
