## Role

Jenna Ross holds the **ARB Secretariat** function for the Payments
Architecture Review Board (ARB) at Crestline National Bank — the board
that issues architecture decisions binding on channel, payments, and
data teams. In that capacity she is recorded as the **document owner**
of [ARB-PAY-REG-2026Q3](../documents/arb-pay-reg-2026q3.md), the
published 2026-Q3 extract combining the Payments ADR register with the
decision minutes of the board's 2026-09-08 session.

## Secretariat responsibilities

As document owner, Ross is accountable for the register-and-minutes
artifact itself — its accuracy, versioning, and publication — rather
than for the architecture decisions it records, which are authored and
approved by the board and its Chair. The secretariat function she
performs is the administrative and process backbone of the ARB
described in
[Payments ARB / Enterprise Architecture](../teams/payments-arb-enterprise-architecture.md):
maintaining the ADR register, recording decision minutes for each
session, and managing the board's intake and scheduling process.

The ARB-PAY-REG-2026Q3 extract that Ross owns itself documents that
process: it sets the cadence for upcoming sessions (2026-11-10 and
2026-12-08) and states that submissions for board review are due **10
business days** prior via the ARB intake form — the kind of
secretariat-run mechanism that keeps the register and minutes current
between quarterly publications.

## Relationship to the ADR register and minutes

ARB-PAY-REG-2026Q3, published 2026-09-12 and marked "uncontrolled when
printed" (the copy in document control is authoritative), is the
vehicle through which Ross's secretariat work reaches channel, payments,
and data teams. It is a combined artifact:

- **Section 1 — ADR register:** the authoritative, currently-binding
  list of Payments ADRs (ADR-PAY-017, -019, -021, -023, -026), each
  carrying a status, applicability, and date.
- **Section 2 — minutes:** the record of agenda items, discussion, and
  decisions from the 2026-09-08 ARB session (PPH v1 sunset, the gpi
  real-time service funding decision, status-projection-pattern
  reaffirmation, and structured address enforcement), each with a named
  business owner distinct from Ross.

Ross is not the owner of any individual ADR or minutes item — those are
attributed to subject-matter owners such as Kevin O'Brien, Elena
Vasquez, Arjun Mehta, and Raymond Ortiz — but she is the point of
accountability for the document as a published, versioned governance
artifact. See
[ARB-PAY-REG-2026Q3](../documents/arb-pay-reg-2026q3.md) for the full
content of the register and minutes she owns.
