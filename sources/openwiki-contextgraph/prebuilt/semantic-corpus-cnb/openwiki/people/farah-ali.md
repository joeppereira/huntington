## Overview

Farah Ali is the accountable leader for **Information Security - Digital Channels**, a first/second-line partner function to Payments & Treasury Technology (PTT). She is the named point of contact for **SECREV**, the information security design review that product and engineering teams must route client-facing and payment-system changes through before go-live.

## Role and scope

Within the PTT organization, engineering and product teams are directed to identify accountable owners for cross-team dependencies and to raise demand through the correct intake route. Farah Ali is listed as the accountable owner of the Information Security - Digital Channels partner function, with design reviews (SECREV) as the specific engagement surface her function provides to PTT teams.

Unlike the engineering team leaders in the PTT directory (who own specific systems such as SYS-CBO or SYS-PPH), Farah Ali's function is advisory/control-oriented: it does not own a system in the PTT system ownership register, but it gates delivery of systems owned by others whenever a design touches digital channel security posture.

## Engagement: SECREV design reviews

Teams needing a security design review engage Information Security (SECREV) under the following planning factors:

- **Effort**: a SECREV design review is estimated at roughly 24-40 engineering/security hours.
- **Lead time / SLA**: reviews are serviced under a 3-week SLA.

Because SECREV is listed among the engagement routes and planning factors used for first-pass sizing of cross-team dependencies, teams planning work that requires a security design review should budget both the review effort and the 3-week SLA into their delivery timeline, alongside other required governance steps (e.g., Architecture Review Board, Privacy Impact Assessment, or Disclosure Review Committee approvals) when a change also touches those domains.

## Relationships

- **Peer partner functions**: Information Security - Digital Channels sits alongside other first/second-line partner functions in the PTT directory, including Financial Crimes Compliance (Catherine Doyle), Model Risk Management (Jonathan Price), Payment Operations (Denise Carter), Legal - Treasury & Payments (Andrew Feldman), Privacy Office & Data Governance (Rachel Goldberg), and Deposits Data Ownership (Mark Sullivan).
- **Consumers of SECREV**: Any PTT engineering team delivering a client-facing or digital-channel feature - for example the CBO Wire Center squad, CBO Platform & Entitlements, or Payments Hub Engineering - may need to route a design through SECREV ahead of go-live, in addition to any architecture, model-risk, or privacy approvals the change also triggers.
