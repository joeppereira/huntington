## Overview

Andrew Feldman is the accountable leader for **Legal - Treasury & Payments**, one of the first- and second-line partner functions listed alongside Payments & Treasury Technology (PTT) engineering teams in the PTT Organization, System Ownership & Engagement Directory. He represents Legal's interests for treasury and payments products and engineering work, distinct from the engineering teams that own systems directly.

## Role and scope

- **Function**: Legal - Treasury & Payments (a partner function, not an engineering team).
- **Accountable**: Andrew Feldman is named as the accountable owner of this function in the PTT engagement directory's partner-function table.
- **Contact for product/design reviews**: Patricia Moore, who chairs the Disclosure Review Committee (DRC), is listed as the contact for product and design reviews under Feldman's function.

Legal - Treasury & Payments sits in the same partner-function tier as Financial Crimes Compliance (FCC), Model Risk Management (MRM), Payment Operations, the Commercial Service Center, Information Security - Digital Channels, the Privacy Office & Data Governance, and Deposits Data Ownership. These functions are accountable second-line (or legal/compliance) partners that engineering teams must engage through defined routes rather than owning systems themselves.

## Engagement route: Disclosure Review Committee

The primary engagement route into Feldman's function, as documented in the directory, runs through the **Disclosure Review Committee (DRC)**, chaired by Patricia Moore:

- **Cadence**: Bi-weekly, Thursdays.
- **Submission lead time**: 5 business days.
- **Scope of approval**: Client-facing copy, disclaimers, and notification templates.

The DRC is a recurring checkpoint for teams across PTT whenever client-facing wording needs legal sign-off, including:

- New Enterprise Notification Service (ENS) event types, where draft templates per channel must be submitted to the DRC as a required step in the event-onboarding workflow before production enablement (SLA: 6 weeks from complete submission to production, including DRC review).
- Aggregated cohort statistics shown to clients, where the wording and disclaimer accompanying any cohort statistic must be approved by the DRC as one of the mandatory conditions under the Data Use & Client Confidentiality Standard (DUS-07) before the statistic may be displayed.

Because the DRC meets only bi-weekly and requires 5 business days' lead time, teams planning client-facing notification or disclosure changes need to factor DRC review into their schedules; missing a cycle adds roughly two weeks of delay.

## Relationships

- Reports into the Legal function serving Payments & Treasury (not an engineering reporting line); engages PTT engineering and product teams as a first/second-line partner rather than an owning team.
- Works alongside Patricia Moore (DRC Chair) as the designated contact for day-to-day product/design review engagement.
- Peer partner-function leaders in the same directory include Catherine Doyle (Financial Crimes Compliance), Jonathan Price (Model Risk Management), Denise Carter (Payment Operations), Kim Nguyen (Commercial Service Center), Farah Ali (Information Security - Digital Channels), Rachel Goldberg (Privacy Office & Data Governance), and Mark Sullivan (Deposits Data Ownership).
- Escalation path: compliance or policy interpretation questions are explicitly routed to FCC Policy & Advisory or the Privacy Office, not decided unilaterally by engineering teams; Legal's disclosure-review role operates within this same governance model of accountable, named approvers for non-engineering concerns.
