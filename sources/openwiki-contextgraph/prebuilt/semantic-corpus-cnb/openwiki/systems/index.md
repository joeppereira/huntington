# Files

- [CBO Status Projection Service (SPS) (SYS-CBO-SPS)](cbo-status-projection-service.md) - Reusable Kafka-consumer-to-Postgres-read-model system (SYS-CBO-SPS, built under CBO-3815) that is CNB's reference pattern for event-driven, non-polling payment status in Crestline Business Online; today configured for ACH only and not wired to Wire Center's Fedwire/Swift wires.
- [CBO Wire Center (SYS-CBO)](cbo-wire-center.md) - Current-state architecture of the Wire Center module in Crestline Business Online (CBO) - the commercial-portal UI, BFF, and synchronous PPH v1 / CES / CBO Auth / ENS integrations behind domestic and international wire initiation, status display, and notifications - including what it explicitly does not support today (milestone tracking, gpi status, hold explanations, client actions on holds).
- [Commercial Entitlements Service (CES) (SYS-CES)](commercial-entitlements-service.md) - Tier 1 authorization and entitlements system owned by the CBO Platform team that gates Wire Center actions and filters results to a user's entitled accounts, and whose unresolved account-filter mapping to PPH v2 search (CBO-4473) blocks the Wire Center PPH v1-to-v2 migration (CBO-4471).
- [Core Deposit Platform (CDP)](core-deposit-platform.md) - CNB's system of record for demand deposit accounts (DDAs), providing the funds-control checks (hold reason HRC-05) consumed by PRISM Payments Hub and the daily dda_txn_history feed consumed by the Treasury Data & Insights Platform; account-level data is classified Restricted - Client Confidential.
- [Enterprise Notification Service (ENS) (SYS-ENS)](enterprise-notification-service.md) - Shared multi-channel notification platform (email, SMS, mobile push, CBO in-app inbox) that delivers producer-emitted events through registered templates and per-user subscriptions; covers its event-onboarding lifecycle, subscription-model limits, and the current Treasury (TRS) wire/ACH event catalog gaps relevant to wire tracking.
- [Fedwire Funds Connector (FFC) (SYS-PNG-FFC)](fedwire-funds-connector.md) - PNE-owned Fedwire module of the Payment Network Gateway handling FedLine Direct connectivity, ISO 20022 message construction, pacs.002/OMAD acknowledgment publishing, and the two net.fedwire.* Kafka topics; the one PNG module that stayed with Payment Networks Engineering in the 2026-10-01 cross-border re-org.
- [Hold Management Service (HMS) (SYS-PRSP-HMS)](hold-management-service.md) - System of record for payment holds at Crestline National Bank — the HRC hold-reason taxonomy, hold lifecycle states, disclosure tiers, and the data-handling controls (CTRL-PAY-012/018/031/040) that govern what any channel may ever learn or show about a hold.
- [Investigations Workbench (IWB) (SYS-IWB)](investigations-workbench.md) - Payment Operations Tech tool that surfaces Hold Management Service (HMS) hold queues and Swift gpi tracking data to Payment Operations and the FIU, and is today the only system that shows international (gpi) wire status anywhere at CNB.
- [PRISM Payments Hub (PPH) (SYS-PPH)](prism-payments-hub.md) - CNB's on-premises wire orchestration platform (vendor Volaris Payment Platform 9.4) that intakes, screens, funds-controls, releases, and settles Fedwire, Swift CBPR+, and book-transfer payments through a ten-state v2 canonical lifecycle, the coarser v1 status model it is replacing, and the fixed 2027-03-31 v1 sunset tied to the Volaris 9.6 upgrade.
- [SanctionScreen (SYS-PRSP-SSC)](sanctionscreen.md) - OFAC and other sanctions-list screening system within the Payment Risk & Screening Platform (PRSP) at Crestline National Bank; its potential-match hits drive HRC-09 SANCTIONS_REVIEW holds and the BLOCKED/REJECTED dispositions that carry OFAC regulatory reporting obligations.
- [Sentinel Fraud Scoring (vendor) (SYS-PRSP-SEN)](sentinel-fraud-scoring.md) - Third-party vendor fraud-scoring model (MRM ID M-FCT-0021) inside the Payment Risk & Screening Platform whose above-threshold scores drive HMS's HRC-07 FRAUD_MODEL_HIGH payment hold, and whose outputs are permanently barred from product, marketing, or client-facing use under MRM-POL-02's Tier 1 financial-crimes restriction.
- [Swift Alliance & gpi Connector (GPI-C) (SYS-PNG-GPI)](swift-gpi-connector.md) - Swift-facing module of the Payment Network Gateway handling CBPR+ ISO 20022 messaging and a quota-constrained 4-hourly batch pull of Swift gpi Tracker status into GPI_TRACKER_SNAPSHOT; ownership transferred from Payment Networks Engineering to GTSI effective 2026-10-01.
- [Treasury Data & Insights Platform (TDIP)](treasury-data-insights-platform.md) - Crestline National Bank's Snowflake/dbt/Feast/SageMaker analytics platform for the Payments domain, which curates wire and deposit datasets, builds feature tables, and serves client-facing analytics exclusively through the Insights API; its wire-data freshness and ingestion gaps currently block any real-time or settlement-time wire-prediction feature.
