# CNB Fair Representation Standard (FRS-02)

Internal CNB standard cited in [POL-FCC-014](pol-fcc-014.md) (regulatory/standards
basis table): client-facing **estimates and status language must be accurate** — the
fair-representation counterpart to Section 5 of the FTC Act (UDAP: representations to
clients must not be unfair or deceptive).

Relevant to any wire-tracking feature that shows predicted delivery times, corridor
statistics, or status wording to clients; see also [DUS-07](dus-07.md) (data use and
client confidentiality) and [MRM-POL-02](mrm-pol-02.md) (model risk tiering for
prediction features).
